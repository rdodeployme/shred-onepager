01The gapWhy difficult material stalls

The material nobody quotes.

Waste contractors are set up for predictable streams. Clean cardboard. Sorted plastics. General waste. The moment a material is bonded, oversized, contaminated or carrying your brand, it falls outside the service. It gets quoted as landfill, or it doesn’t get quoted at all.

Meanwhile it sits in your racking, and the cost of it never appears on a waste invoice.

01

Brand exposure

Product that reaches a landfill floor intact can be pulled off it, resold, or photographed with your logo still on it.

02

Warehouse drag

Rejected stock ties up racking, floor space and forklift hours for months while someone works out what to do with it. That someone has a day job.

03

Reporting gaps

Landfill disposal produces no recovery data. If you report on diversion, packaging obligations or Scope 3, unprocessed material is a hole in the numbers.

04

Escalating cost

Landfill levies go up every year, and mixed waste pays the highest gate rate in the schedule.

Where it otherwise ends: mixed waste, at the highest gate rate in the schedule.

04Chain of custodyFor recalled, branded & defective product

Gone, and provable.

With recalled, branded or defective product, the real risk is the product turning up again. The disposal cost is a footnote. SHRED runs these projects as a controlled chain, and what you get back is a documented outcome, not a weighbridge docket.

Your quality, legal and sustainability people all want the same thing: proof of what happened to it. A weighbridge docket doesn’t give them that. Processing records do.

The controlled chain

  1. 01
    Sealed collectionMaterial is loaded, secured and manifested at your site with item counts recorded against the agreed scope.
  2. 02
    Direct to processingConsignments are directed to the nominated processing site without intermediate handling or resale exposure.
  3. 03
    Irreversible size reductionProduct is shredded or granulated to a state where the item cannot be reconstituted, identified or resold.
  4. 04
    Documented outcomeYou receive processing records covering quantity, method and destination for each consignment.
